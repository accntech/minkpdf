# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MinkPDF aims to be a welcoming, respectful project where people can contribute and learn. Everyone
participating is expected to help maintain an environment free from harassment, regardless of age,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual
orientation, experience, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of people with different perspectives, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code. Explain disagreements without attacking people.
- Accept feedback in good faith, acknowledge mistakes, and make corrections when needed.
- Respect other people's privacy, time, and boundaries.
- Help keep issues, pull requests, and other discussions focused and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, discriminatory remarks, or personal insults.
- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Deliberate trolling, repeated disruption, or sustained hostility toward a person or group.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern or participating in its review.
- Other conduct that undermines a respectful and safe community.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions. It
also applies when someone represents MinkPDF in another space, such as through an official project
account or at a project event. Maintainers and contributors are held to the same standards.

## Report a concern

Contact the maintainers privately at [accntech.dev@gmail.com](mailto:accntech.dev@gmail.com).
Include links or screenshots where available, a description of what happened, and any information
that would help the maintainers respond. Share only what you feel comfortable sharing. Do not open a
public issue containing a conduct report or another person's private information.

If a concern involves a maintainer, identify that conflict in your report. Anyone directly involved
in a complaint should step aside from reviewing it; another uninvolved maintainer should handle the
report where available. You can also report abuse on GitHub directly through
[GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam),
including concerns about a maintainer or cases where no uninvolved maintainer is available. GitHub
handles those reports under its own policies.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and handle them as privately as possible. Information will be
shared only as needed to investigate and respond. Reporter identities and sensitive details should
not be published without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit content, close or lock conversations, or restrict participation when
behavior violates this code. Responses should reflect the severity, impact, and any repeated behavior:

- **Correction:** explain the problem and ask for the behavior to stop or for content to be corrected.
- **Warning:** state the violation, the expected change, and the consequences of further violations.
- **Temporary restriction:** suspend participation for a stated period when harm is serious or
  behavior continues after a warning.
- **Permanent restriction:** exclude participants for severe harassment, threats, or repeated
  violations that make continued participation unsafe.

Serious incidents may require immediate restrictions without a prior warning. Where practical,
maintainers will explain decisions privately to the affected participants. Requests to reconsider a
decision may be sent to the same reporting email with relevant context; an uninvolved maintainer
should review them where available.
